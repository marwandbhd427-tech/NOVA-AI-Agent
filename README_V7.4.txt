NOVA V7.4.0 — VERIFIED RESEARCH

Major change:
- Evidence-first web research.
- Extracts song/entity title from natural-language questions.
- Runs multiple independent searches and domain-targeted searches.
- Collects up to 10 evidence results.
- Scores trusted music sources.
- Requires two independent strong source domains before treating a fact as verified.
- If evidence is insufficient or conflicting, NOVA must not guess.
- LLM is instructed and gated to use retrieved evidence only.

Recommended tests:
1) ابحث عن اغنية belbala
2) من هو فنان اغنية Belbala؟
3) ابحث عن اغنية Bali Maak وتحقق من الفنان وتاريخ الإصدار من أكثر من مصدر
4) ما هي أغنية XYZABC123؟
5) هل توجد أكثر من أغنية باسم Belbala؟
